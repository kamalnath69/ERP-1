# Outpatient clinic

The Clinic workspace supports patient queues, encounters, vitals, diagnoses, prescriptions, labs, allergies, and pharmacy dispensing.

## Clinical permissions

Clinical viewing, writing, signing, and dispensing are separate capabilities. Administrative access does not automatically grant access to clinical content.

## Encounter workflow

Record source-backed observations and maintain responsible staff attribution. Signed clinical records should use correction or amendment workflows rather than silent replacement.

## AI limitations

Edvatiq AI can summarize authorized records but does not replace a qualified practitioner. Verify all clinical information and never use an AI response as the sole basis for diagnosis or treatment.
