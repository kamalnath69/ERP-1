# Edvatiq documentation

Edvatiq combines focused operations, College placement intelligence, and evidence-backed AI in one permission-aware workspace.

## What Edvatiq is for

- Gym and fitness teams managing memberships, attendance, coaching, and billing.
- Salon and spa teams managing appointments, client preferences, checkout, and inventory.
- Outpatient clinics managing queues, encounters, prescriptions, labs, and pharmacy workflows.
- Colleges managing placement readiness, coding evidence, drives, applications, interviews, offers, and interventions.

Edvatiq does not assume every organization needs the same dashboard. Navigation, terminology, permissions, and records adapt to the configured industry.

## Start here

1. Complete the organization profile and primary location.
2. Invite staff and assign the smallest suitable role.
3. Import or create the records needed for one high-value workflow.
4. Review permissions before enabling AI actions or integrations.
5. Use audit history to verify important changes.

## Evidence-backed assistance

Ask Edvatiq can summarize authorized records, explain counts, compare cohorts, and suggest next actions. Answers must still be reviewed before professional, clinical, financial, academic, or employment decisions.

> Missing evidence is shown as missing. Edvatiq does not silently convert unavailable data into zero.
