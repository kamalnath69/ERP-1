# College placement intelligence

Edvatiq complements the College ERP instead of replacing it.

## Ownership boundary

The ERP normally remains authoritative for student identity, department, program, batch, term results, attendance, and internship-clearance evidence. Edvatiq owns placement assessments, coding profiles, resumes, verified skills, projects, readiness, interventions, companies, drives, applications, interviews, offers, and outcomes.

## Readiness

Readiness combines available academics, coding, assessment, profile, attendance, and training evidence under a versioned policy. Missing factors are excluded from the score and reduce evidence coverage. Protected attributes are excluded from scoring, ranking, eligibility, and recommendations.

## Eligibility

Eligibility is `eligible`, `ineligible`, or `needs_review`. Internship opportunities require a current `cleared` internship-clearance snapshot. `pending` blocks eligibility; missing or stale evidence requires review.

## Placement pipeline

The default pipeline runs from Eligible through Joined, Rejected, or Withdrawn. Placement Heads can configure stages. Stage moves, overrides, interviews, offers, and outcomes retain audit history.

## Student profile

Profiles organize Overview, Academic evidence, Coding and skills, Placements, and Activity. Fee amounts are not part of normal placement workflows; placement staff see only the clearance signal and source freshness.
