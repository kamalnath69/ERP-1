# Edvatiq AI

Edvatiq AI is an assistant over records the current user is authorized to access.

## Good questions

- Which clients need a payment follow-up?
- Explain why this student needs eligibility review.
- Compare placement readiness across two departments.
- Which inventory items are below their reorder level?
- Summarize this patient's permitted encounter history.

## Language and personalization

The current message determines English, Tamil, or Tanglish for that response. Saved tone, detail, and formatting preferences affect presentation only. They cannot weaken permissions, alter values, bypass confirmation, or change tool scope.

## Evidence and actions

Answers can include record cards and links to supporting data. Actions that change records require explicit confirmation. Treat recommendations as assistance, not as the sole authority for clinical, financial, academic, employment, or legal decisions.

## Conversation controls

Chats can be searched, pinned, renamed, archived, restored, or deleted within the configured retention period. Copy and response feedback controls are available on assistant messages.
