# Plans, billing, and AI credits

Public pricing is loaded from the current published plan catalog. Edvatiq does not display guessed prices when the catalog is unavailable.

## Registration

When trials are enabled, a workspace can be created after legal acceptance and email verification. When trials are disabled, the workspace and owner are created only after the configured payment provider confirms the first-term payment.

## Subscription billing

Plan and billing shows the current subscription, available upgrades, AI wallet, top-up packs, and compact billing history. Tax snapshots on existing invoices remain unchanged when organization tax settings later change.

## AI credits

AI credits belong to the workspace and may expire according to the purchased pack or plan. Failed or cancelled reservations should release unconsumed credits according to the recorded provider outcome.

## Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed under the current [Refund and Cancellation Policy](/refund-policy). Provider processing and bank settlement times may vary.
