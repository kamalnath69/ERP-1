# Salon and spa

The Salon workspace connects appointment flow, services, client preferences, checkout, follow-ups, staff schedules, and stock.

## Client context

Use profiles for contact details, service history, preferences, formulas, notes, appointments, and invoices. Private notes remain permission scoped.

## Daily operation

Calendar and work queues show upcoming bookings and actions. Catalog defines services and products, while inventory tracks consumables and retail stock by location.

## Checkout

Sales records retain the customer, item names, totals, payments, balance, and invoice status. Correct records through permitted payment or void workflows rather than deleting history.
