# Core modules

Core modules appear according to industry, subscription, and permission.

## People records

Clients are called students in College workspaces and patients where clinical context requires it. Profiles bring together identity, permitted operational history, billing or clearance state, and industry-specific evidence.

## Calendar and work

Calendar manages bounded appointment ranges and role-appropriate actions. Work queues surface tasks that need ownership without filling empty pages with placeholder records.

## Catalog, inventory, and sales

Catalog defines products and services. Inventory tracks location stock and movements. Sales connects invoices, line items, customers, payments, and outstanding balances.

## Documents and notifications

Documents are validated before storage and processing. Notifications retain delivery state and respect communication permissions. Never upload credentials or secrets as ordinary documents.

## Reports

Reports render only useful charts for the selected range. Empty data is explained once rather than repeated across oversized panels.
