# Gym and fitness

The Gym workspace focuses on membership lifecycle, check-ins, coaching, progress, classes, workouts, diet plans, and equipment.

## Membership lifecycle

Activation and renewal create linked invoices. Staff choose full payment, partial payment, or pay later. Early renewal creates a scheduled next term while the current term remains active.

## Billing clarity

Profiles separate plan status, term charge, paid value, and outstanding balance. Outstanding invoices show item names and payment actions. Only fully unpaid invoices can be voided, with a reason and audit entry.

## Attendance and coaching

Check-ins, measurements, workouts, coaching notes, and classes are visible only when relevant data and permissions exist. Empty optional sections remain hidden.
